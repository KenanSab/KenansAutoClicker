# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. This is a small project built to make computers easier to
use; it costs nothing to be kind in it.

## What's expected

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are misunderstandings.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Accept that maintainers have limited time, and that "no" is a valid answer.
- Remember that many users here have a disability or an injury. Do not treat
  accessibility needs as an edge case or an inconvenience.

## What's not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, sustained disruption, publishing
private information, or unwelcome personal attention. Submitting presets or code
intended to harm users, such as anything that hides what it does, also counts.

## Reporting

Open an issue, or contact the maintainer through
[github.com/KenanSab](https://github.com/KenanSab).

Reports are read in confidence. Responses range from a request to stop, through
edits or removal of content, to blocking an account, depending on severity.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and anywhere
someone is representing the project.
